Given an upstream gene set and a downstream gene set, the application identifies intermediate regulators statistically enriched in both directions, assembles three-step paths (upstream gene → intermediate → downstream gene), and scores them by a normalized product of enrichment strength and effect sizes from both datasets.

Upload two files containing your upstream and downstream gene sets. Each file must contain a column with gene symbols and a column with a ranking metric. Metric values will be scaled to [0, 1] so that upstream and downstream scores are comparable regardless of their original units.
